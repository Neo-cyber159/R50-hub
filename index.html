💳 Payment instructions

Protect your banking information. Never publish your account number, card number, PIN, password, OTP, or banking login on this website.

How payment should work

  1. Customer and worker agree on the job and price.
  2. Customer pays using an approved payment method.
  3. Only share payment details privately and only when appropriate.
  4. Confirm that the payment has actually arrived before treating a job as paid.

Your payment setup

R50 Hub does not store or display your bank details. If you use a GoTyme account, confirm with GoTyme that your particular account can receive payments for the work you offer. If a business account or adult/guardian involvement is required, follow GoTyme's rules.

Scam warning: Never send money back because a customer shows a screenshot claiming they overpaid. Check your actual account balance/transaction history, and never share an OTP to “release” a payment.

Safety checklist

Never share passwords, OTPs, banking PINs, home addresses or identity documents. Never pay to unlock a job. Don't accept unsafe, illegal or age-restricted work. If a payment platform requires an adult account, use a parent/guardian.
